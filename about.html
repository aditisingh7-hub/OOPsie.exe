About UniFest

Our College Event Registration Portal is designed to make discovering and registering for college events easier. From technical competitions to cultural activities, the platform brings all events together in one place.

Students can explore upcoming events, learn about them and register conveniently, making campus participation more accessible and engaging.

Our Objectives

Discover Talent

Encourage students to explore and showcase their unique abilities.

Build Confidence

Promote participation, self-expression and meaningful connections.

Easy Registration

Make exploring and signing up for events simple and convenient.
